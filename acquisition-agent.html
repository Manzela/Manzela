Acquisition Agent

This system was built so Jonathan could cover more ground on value-add open-air retail without the usual research friction. The file he reviews is already checked, and nothing reaches him until he has said yes.

What changed for him

Before this system existed, county research meant incomplete records, mixed data sources, and spreadsheets that still needed manual cleanup. Jonathan spent time checking rows instead of deciding which properties were worth pursuing. The system now runs the research in the background, keeps a clear trail on every property, and only surfaces what has passed the checks. He can see the status of a run without asking, and provisional items stay out of the delivery until someone reviews them.

Why it is hard to copy

The edge is not a smarter model. It is persistent specialist agents plus a shared trail that cannot be silently rewritten, plus hard stops before any file leaves. Provisional exclusions stay provisional until reviewed. Sequence numbers and phase closes show exactly what was done and what remains. The approval is explicit and in the loop rather than an afterthought.
